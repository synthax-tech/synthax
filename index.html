Frequently Asked Questions

If you can't find the answer you are looking for, feel free to contact us anytime!

Who can participate?

Students from 6th to 12th grade who are passionate about coding, technology, and innovation.

Do I need my own equipment?

Bring your laptop and peripherals. Internet access, workspace, and meals will be provided at the venue.

Are hardware projects allowed?

Yes, both software and hardware projects are welcome. Bring your ideas to life!

Can beginners join?

Absolutely! All skill levels are encouraged to participate and learn through hands-on experience.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes, there is a registration fee of ₹100 per participant.

When and where is the event?

The hackathon will take place in Bangalore on 22-23 November 2025 at Polaris School of Technology- DivyaSree Technopark, EPIP Zone, Brookefield

Team size and participation?

Teams can have 2-4 participants. Solo participants can reach out to find teammates.

What if I have more questions?

Feel free to contact us anytime. Our team will guide you through any queries.
